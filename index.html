Quantum Baboon

Run on your hands, explore the Backrooms with friends and escape the Bacteria.

Quantum Baboon is a multiplayer VR game for Meta Quest where you move like a gorilla: push off the floor and walls with your hands to run, jump and climb. Meet your friends in the lobby, then step through the portal into the Backrooms, endless yellow halls where something called the Bacteria is hunting.

  • Meta Quest
  • Online multiplayer, up to 10 players
  • Arm-based locomotion
  • Comfort: Intense
  • Early Access

Hands, not joysticks

Your arms are your legs. Push, swing and climb your way around.

Play together

Join public rooms or make a private code for you and your friends.

The Backrooms

Explore the halls, stay quiet and get back out before the Bacteria finds you.
